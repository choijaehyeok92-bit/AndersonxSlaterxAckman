# Cerebras Systems (NASDAQ: CBRS) — long-term (5–10 yr) equity research fact base, as of 2026-10-18

Conventions: every figure is dated. "[>6 mo old]" marks items dated before 2026-04-18. "Estimate:" marks my own arithmetic, which is not company-reported. Primary sources (SEC filings) are used wherever I could reach them. Secondary sources are labelled.

## 1. IPO details and post-IPO results (Q1 and Q2 FY2026), next report and guidance

### Takeaway
Cerebras IPO'd on 2026-05-13 at $185, raising about $6.4B gross including the over-allotment. Two public quarters show core (non-GAAP) revenue roughly doubling, a fast shift from hardware to cloud, and core results ahead of guidance. But Q2 GAAP gross margin fell to 14.2% and GAAP operating loss was $477M, both driven by IPO-related SBC and customer-warrant contra-revenue. Q3 FY2026 results (quarter ending 2026-09-30) are expected in early-to-mid November 2026, and the main lock-up expiry is tied to that release.

## 2. Customer concentration: G42/MBZUAI and the newer large customers

### Takeaway
Concentration has rotated rather than fallen. In FY2025, UAE-linked MBZUAI (62%) and G42 (24%) were 86% of revenue. In Q2 FY2026, a related-party customer pair (A, likely MBZUAI/G42) was 34% and Customer B, which matches OpenAI, was 32%. The $25.4B RPO is mostly the OpenAI Master Relationship Agreement (MRA). OpenAI is also Cerebras's secured lender ($918M) and largest warrant holder.

### Inferences
- Customer B = OpenAI is an inference: $56.8M / $180.1M = 31.5%, about 32%. Customer A = MBZUAI (related to D = G42) is also an inference: A held 78% of receivables at year-end 2025, in line with MBZUAI's 77.9% in the S-1.
- Estimate: in Q2 2026 the top three customers were about 76% of revenue. The UAE share is falling (A was 70% in Q2 2025, 34% in Q2 2026), but OpenAI exposure is rising and dominates the backlog.
- The receivables jump the harness red team flagged (from $62.6M to $123.4M) is mostly OpenAI (Customer B, 57% of receivables). That points to an OpenAI ramp/billing timing story, not a UAE collection problem, though it is still a concentration risk.
- RPO timing: estimate about $5.6B (22%) to be recognized by mid-2028, an average of about $2.8B a year. That is roughly in line with management's "more than triple in 2027" (estimate: about $2.6–2.7B on the FY26 core guide). The other 78% of the backlog sits after mid-2028 and depends on OpenAI's continued take-up.
- OpenAI holds three roles at once: the largest RPO counterparty, the secured lender with an acceleration clause, and the warrant holder. That gives it unusual bargaining power.

## 3. GAAP vs "core" margins: what the adjustments are

### Takeaway
The gap the harness could not explain is now explained by the primary filing. In Q2, core gross profit ($85.2M) exceeded GAAP ($25.6M) by about $59.6M:
- $44.3M of non-cash customer-warrant amortization, booked as contra-revenue
- $15.4M of SBC in cost of revenue
- $0.5M of IPO payroll tax
- minus about $0.4M net of pass-through items

The opex gap is mostly a one-time $273.6M IPO RSU catch-up. The warrant contra-revenue, however, is recurring: a $1.13B warrant asset amortizes through October 2031.

### Cited Findings
- Core measures "exclude the impacts of non-cash amortization of customer warrants and stock-based compensation", plus pass-through items and certain other items. Q2 revenue bridge: GAAP $180.1M − pass-through revenue $14.5M + warrant amortization $44.3M = core $209.9M. The warrant amortization split was $28.0M hardware and $16.2M cloud. Pass-through revenue relates to "non-recurring data center start-up" costs billed on behalf of specific customers — [8-K Q2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002021728/000162828026056186/cbrsannouncesfinancialresu.htm)
- Gross-profit reconciling items in Q2: pass-through costs $14.1M; warrant amortization $44.3M; SBC in cost of revenue $15.4M; employer payroll tax on IPO-related SBC $0.5M — [8-K Q2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002021728/000162828026056186/cbrsannouncesfinancialresu.htm)
- Operating reconciling items in Q2: total SBC $377.0M; warrant amortization $44.3M; pass-through costs $14.1M; payroll tax on IPO SBC $22.8M; less pass-through revenue $14.5M. GAAP opex was $502.8M vs core opex $118.8M — [8-K Q2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002021728/000162828026056186/cbrsannouncesfinancialresu.htm)
- $273.6M of Q2 SBC was for RSUs whose liquidity condition was satisfied at the IPO on 2026-05-13, a one-time catch-up. 6M 2026 SBC was $386.6M vs $22.4M in 6M 2025. Outstanding at 2026-06-30: 23.8M options, 16.5M unvested RSUs and 9.0M contingently issuable PRSUs — [10-Q Q2 FY26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002021728/000162828026056357/cbrs-20260630.htm)
- Customer warrant assets at 2026-06-30 were $167.8M current and $960.6M non-current, amortizing as a reduction of revenue through October 2031. Amortization was $44.3M in Q2 and $46.3M in 6M 2026, so about $2.0M in Q1 — [10-Q Q2 FY26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002021728/000162828026056357/cbrs-20260630.htm)
- SemiAnalysis estimated a theoretical maximum OpenAI warrant contra-revenue of about $2.74B at $82.02 per share, roughly 10% of expected OpenAI revenue — [SemiAnalysis, 2026-05-13](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/cerebras-faster-tokens-please)
- Q1 FY2026 had only a small gap (GAAP gross margin 45% vs core 47%) — [8-K Q1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002021728/000162828026044941/cbrsannouncesfinancialresu.htm)
- FY2025 GAAP net income of $237.8M included a one-time non-cash gain of $363.3M on extinguishing a G42-related forward contract. FY2025 non-GAAP net loss was $75.7M and SBC $49.8M — [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/cerebras-s-1-teardown-is-the-23b-wafer-scale-ipo-the-end-of-gpu-homogeneity/); net income $237,827K in the [424B4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002021728/000162828026035214/cerebras-424b4.htm). DCD's "$87.9M net income" — [DCD, 2026-07-09](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/cerebras-to-deploy-200mw-of-compute-in-europe-by-end-of-2027/) — conflicts with the filing; use the 424B4 figure.

### Inferences
- The rise in warrant amortization from about $2M in Q1 to $44.3M in Q2 only partly relates to OpenAI ($2.5M in Q2). About $41.8M relates to other customers (estimate), possibly warrants granted to AWS or others, or IPO-driven revaluation. This is unresolved and matters: it hit hardware revenue by $28.0M, which suggests a hardware customer.

## 5. Competition and technology: WSE vs NVIDIA (incl. Groq), Groq, TPU, Trainium, AMD; benchmarks; TSMC

### Takeaway
Cerebras still leads on single-stream decode speed for small and mid-size models in vendor data. Nvidia's December 2025 Groq deal and the Groq 3 LPX (Artificial Analysis: 3,400 tok/s vs Cerebras 882 on Gemma 4 31B, with caveats) directly contest that niche. Structural critiques from SemiAnalysis are SRAM capacity stalled at 44 GB, weak off-wafer I/O and poor economics for 1T+ parameter models. These matter more as frontier models grow. AMD and AWS are partners as well as competitors.

### Cited Findings
- WSE-3: one TSMC N5 wafer at about $20k, 44 GB SRAM, about 21 PB/s bandwidth. Marketed at 125 PFLOPS sparse FP16; SemiAnalysis puts it at about 15.6 PFLOPS dense.
