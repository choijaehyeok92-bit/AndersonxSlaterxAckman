## 1. Latest reported quarter (Q2 2026) and Q3 2026 outlook

### Takeaway
Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-05) beat the company's own guidance clearly. Revenue grew 34%, against a "high-20s" guide. GMV grew 32%, operating income rose 68% to $488M, and FCF margin was 18%. Gross margin fell again, by about 90bp YoY to 47.7%, because Payments-led Merchant Solutions keeps growing faster than subscriptions. ## 2. Merchant unit economics (cohorts, churn, LTV/CAC, Plus wins, Shop Pay)

### Takeaway
Shopify still discloses no merchant-level retention, churn, net revenue retention (NRR) or customer acquisition cost (CAC). The harness's "unclear" verdict stands. The only cohort data is a 2022 third-party analysis, more than four years old, showing weak survival for small stores and better survival for Plus merchants; Shopify disputed it. Usable proxies: MRR growth of 19% with subscription revenue up 22%; Shop Pay GMV up 53% (secondary source); B2B GMV up 76% (secondary source). A new risk proxy has also appeared: transaction and loan losses are growing much faster than revenue.

### Cited Findings
- Globe and Mail analysis of Store Leads domain data (stale: about autumn 2022; covers the 2019–2021 cohorts):
  - Only about 34% of all Shopify stores survived one year (2019 cohort 36%, 2020 cohort 34%, 2021 cohort 32%).
  - Shopify Plus one-year survival was 80% for the 2019 cohort, 72% for 2020 and 51% for 2021.
  - Shopify said its internal data was "drastically different" but declined to share it.
  - Source: [The Globe and Mail: Shopify's customer retention problem](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-shopify-customer-retention-problem/)
- In 2022, Plus accounted for 31% of MRR. That figure is stale and was cited in the same article — [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-shopify-customer-retention-problem/)
- MRR rose from $185M to $221M (+19%) while subscription revenue grew 22% (Q2 2026) — [Q2 2026 press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001594805/000159480526000046/exhibit991pressreleaseq220.htm)
- Shop Pay GMV +53% and B2B GMV +76% in Q2 2026 (AI summary, secondary) — [Platform Aeronaut](https://transcripts.platformaeronaut.com/summaries/SHOP-2Q26-AI-Summary)
- Enterprise and brand evidence in 2026:
  - Brands named in the Q2 release: Aldo, BarkBox, Carrier, Meta, SKIMS, Supreme, Vuori — [Q2 2026 press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001594805/000159480526000046/exhibit991pressreleaseq220.htm)
  - Gymshark, Monos and Everlane sell in Google AI Mode/Gemini; Keen and Pura Vida use Copilot Checkout — [Shopify, 2026-01-11](https://www.shopify.com/news/ai-commerce-at-scale)
- Credit and risk proxy:
  - Transaction and loan losses were $141M in Q2 2026 vs $80M in Q2 2025 (+76%), and $257M vs $155M for the first half.
  - Loans and MCAs, net (current), were $2,184M at 2026-06-30 vs $1,784M at 2025-12-31.
  - Source: [Shopify 10-Q, Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001594805/000159480526000047/shop-20260630.htm)
- Platform-switching evidence (agency crawl; weak source): about 9.4% of stores run custom/headless builds, but 38.4% of stores with more than $32M revenue do — [Visionary Marketing 2026 platform report (Q1 2026 crawl)](https://visionary-marketing.co.uk/blog/ecommerce-platform-market-share-2026)

### Inferences
- Transaction and loan losses rose from about 3.0% to about 3.9% of revenue (computed: $80M/$2,680M vs $141M/$3,583M). In the first half of 2026 the MCA/loan book grew about 22% (computed). Shopify Capital is a growing credit exposure to small merchants, and its loss rate is rising. That cost sits partly outside gross margin and is a bear point the harness did not capture.
- MRR (+19%) growing slower than subscription revenue (+22%) may reflect pricing and paid trials, or the fact that MRR is measured at the period end. This cannot be resolved without disclosure.
- Shop Pay growth (+53%) well above GMV growth (+32%) supports the case that the checkout network effect is strengthening. That matters for agentic checkout, where Shop Pay is the rail used in Meta Muse (see §3).

### Gaps
- No primary-source NRR, churn, cohort GMV retention, CAC or LTV for any period. No 2024–2026 third-party cohort study was found.
- No merchant count for 2025 or 2026, and no Plus merchant count was found.
- No named large-enterprise migrations for 2026 beyond the brands above (for example, from Salesforce Commerce Cloud or Adobe) were found in this session.

## 3. Agentic/AI commerce: partnerships, products, revenue impact, disintermediation risk

### Takeaway
Shopify has built the widest agentic distribution of any commerce platform. It co-developed the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) with Google, launched Copilot Checkout, Agentic Storefronts and an Agentic plan for non-Shopify brands, put Shop Pay inside Meta Muse, and turned on agent checkout by default. The evidence cuts both ways:
- **Disintermediation:** In January 2026 OpenAI said it would charge merchants 4% on ChatGPT-checkout sales on top of Shopify's fees. In March 2026 OpenAI scaled back Instant Checkout, which ended Shopify-backed native checkout in ChatGPT.
- **Revenue impact:** Disclosure is limited to secondary multipliers (AI traffic 8x, AI orders about 13x YoY in Q1 2026), with no GMV share.
- **Harness gap:** The harness's manual falsification check ("an AI channel drops Shopify from the default checkout path or demands an intermediary fee") arguably already had documented instances before the debate, and the harness evidence did not record them.

### Cited Findings
- 2026-01-11 launches (source: [Shopify, The agentic commerce platform](https://www.shopify.com/news/ai-commerce-at-scale)):
  - UCP, co-developed with Google, is an open standard that works over REST, MCP, AP2 and A2A, and with any payment processor, including Shopify Payments.
  - Agentic Storefronts let merchants syndicate products to ChatGPT, Copilot, Google AI Mode and Gemini from Shopify Admin.
  - Copilot Checkout is an embedded checkout inside Microsoft Copilot.
  - The Agentic plan lets non-Shopify brands list in Shopify Catalog (billions of items) without a Shopify store.
  - The page gives no fees and no AI-order metrics.
- Fees and default settings (source: [PYMNTS, Jan 2026, citing The Information and a Shopify spokesperson](https://www.pymnts.com/news/ecommerce/2026/shopify-merchants-to-pay-4percent-fee-on-sales-made-through-chatgpt-checkout)):
  - Merchants pay OpenAI 4% on ChatGPT-checkout sales "on top of the fees charged by Shopify".
  - Google AI Mode, Gemini and Copilot charge no added fee "for now".
  - Products are on by default only on platforms without an added fee; merchants must opt in to platforms that charge one.
- OpenAI scaled back Instant Checkout and called the change "temporary" (around early March 2026). Details (source: [Forrester blog, ~March 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/what-it-means-that-the-leader-in-agentic-commerce-just-pulled-back)):
  - Checkout moves to merchant apps inside ChatGPT or back to merchant websites.
  - The Shopify-backed native checkout on Shopify Payments, from the late-2025 OpenAI partnership, "is now gone". Shopify still syndicates catalogs to ChatGPT.
  - Only about 12 non-Shopify merchants were live on the catalog service (per The Information); Shopify told Forrester about 30.
  - Completing a purchase inside an answer engine was the least-adopted use case in Forrester survey data.
- Meta Muse (source: [Forkast, 2026-09-22](https://forkast.news/shopify-embraces-metas-muse-across-all-stores-as-amazon-blocks-it-and-the-merchant-landscape-splits-in-two/)):
  - Shop Pay went live in Muse on 2026-09-21. Muse's checkout paths are Shop Pay for Shopify merchants, Stripe Link for others, and browser automation as a fallback.
  - Amazon blocked Muse from its marketplace the same day.
  - Agentic Storefronts (launched December 2025) make every eligible US-selling store discoverable by default.
  - Per Shopify figures for Q1 2026, AI-driven traffic to Shopify stores grew 8x YoY and orders from AI searches about 13x.
  - No public data yet shows Muse/Shop Pay working reliably at scale.
- Covered in the harness and not re-fetched in this session: agent checkout on by default for about a million merchants (2026-09-24, harness S47); checkout opened to browser-based AI agents (2026-09-28, harness S22); catalog opened to more AI agents (2026-10-13, harness S27); Meta Muse partnership (WSJ 2026-09-21, harness S30); Reuters, 2026-08-05: "AI is boosting business, not disrupting" (harness S26).

### Inferences
- **Bull:** Shopify is the default merchant-side rail across Google, Microsoft and Meta, with Shop Pay as the checkout identity. Amazon blocking Muse leaves Shopify as the largest catalog willing to transact with third-party agents. Shop Pay GMV +53% fits that picture.
- **Bear:**
  - The single largest AI surface, ChatGPT, first inserted a 4% toll and then pulled native checkout back to apps and websites. AI platforms, not Shopify, decide whether checkout sits inside the agent and what it costs.
  - "No added fee for now" at Google and Microsoft is explicitly provisional.
  - Interest in the Agentic plan for non-Shopify brands was small as of early 2026 (about 12–30 brands live).
- For the harness: Anderson's manual condition (AI channel removes default checkout or demands an intermediary fee) has two historical instances (ChatGPT 4% fee, Jan 2026; ChatGPT native checkout removed, Mar 2026). Both predate the debate and are not in evidence.json. Neither has yet shown up in reported results: Q2 growth was 34% and penetration rose to 68%. The committee should decide whether this counts as "triggered, but immaterial so far".
- Revenue impact cannot be quantified from disclosures. The 8x and 13x multipliers start from an unknown, likely tiny, base.

### Gaps
- No disclosed share of GMV or orders from AI agents, and no disclosed take rate on agentic orders.
- No source found on Perplexity–Shopify checkout status in 2026.
- No primary confirmation of the "million merchants default-on" figure was fetched in this session (harness S47 only).
- Q3 2026 or holiday agentic metrics are not yet available (due 2026-11-03).

## 4. Competition and market share

### Takeaway
Shopify is the clear number-two US e-commerce "channel" after Amazon. An unsourced 2026 article puts its share at about 14% of 2025 US e-commerce (12% in 2024), and Marketplace Pulse puts Amazon plus Shopify at about 49.7% combined. Shopify is the leading storefront platform by store count. Amazon is moving into the merchant-management layer and blocking third-party agents. Reliable 2026 data on Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Adobe/Magento, BigCommerce, TikTok Shop and Temu was not found.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 US e-commerce shares (source: [EcomCrew, 2026-02-27](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-and-shopify-now-control-nearly-half-of-u-s-e-commerce/)):
  - Amazon 35.7% (about $440B); Forbes cites 37.6%.
  - Shopify 14%, up from 12% in 2024 (source not named).
  - Walmart 6.4%, Apple 3.6%, eBay 3%.
  - Amazon plus Shopify 49.7% combined, credited to Marketplace Pulse, vs about 43% in 2021.
- Store-count shares among about 84,000 detected stores (agency crawl, Feb–Mar 2026; not independently verified; source: [Visionary Marketing](https://visionary-marketing.co.uk/blog/ecommerce-platform-market-share-2026)):
  - Shopify including Plus 42.6% (vs 31.8% in 2022).
  - WooCommerce 24.6% (vs 32.4%); Magento 8.4% (vs 18.6%); custom/headless 9.4%.
  - The same page estimates Shopify's 2025 GMV at about $31.2B, which is far below reported GMV (Q2 2026 alone was $115.6B). Use only the store-count shares.
- Amazon (2026-09-24, harness S25): sellers can manage sales across Amazon, eBay, Shopify, TikTok and Walmart from Seller Central, a move into the multichannel merchant-management layer.
- Amazon blocked Meta's Muse agent on 2026-09-21, while Shopify opened Shop Pay to it — [Forkast, 2026-09-22](https://forkast.news/shopify-embraces-metas-muse-across-all-stores-as-amazon-blocks-it-and-the-merchant-landscape-splits-in-two/)
- 2022 cohort data showed that merchants leaving Shopify mostly moved to WooCommerce (stale) — [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-shopify-customer-retention-problem/)

### Inferences
- Share gains: about 12% to about 14% of US e-commerce in one year, if the figure is accurate, against Shopify GMV growth of about 30% vs high-single-digit US e-commerce growth. This is consistent with the harness view that scale is proven by absolute growth. Treat the 14% figure as directional only because its source is unnamed.
- The moat is shifting from "best storefront builder" toward "largest agent-ready catalog plus checkout identity" (Shop Pay). Amazon's closed approach (blocking agents) and Shopify's open approach make a clear strategic contrast. Which wins depends on how AI platforms monetize, which §3 shows is unsettled.
- Wix, Squarespace and AI site builders compete at the low end. Shopify's 2026 Canvas launch (AI store building, harness S28/S31) is a defensive response.

### Gaps
- No 2026 eMarketer or Digital Commerce 360 share data was retrieved. The 14% figure lacks a named source.
- No 2026 data on Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Adobe Commerce or BigCommerce (Commerce.com) enterprise share, losses to Shopify, or competitive pricing.
- No data on TikTok Shop or Temu GMV overlap with Shopify merchants, or on Buy with Prime adoption among Shopify merchants, in 2026.
